The context of tile instruction dispatch.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/tirx/tile/dispatch.py defines. A DispatchContext gives a lowering the target, the thread scope, the launch parameters, the ranges of the variables, the callbacks and the state shared between dispatches. Its methods request allocations and statements from the lowering, and cache objects across dispatches.
