Index maps.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/tirx/index_map.py defines. An IndexMap maps its initial_indices to its final_indices, as (i, j) -> (j, i) does, and may hold its inverse. Its methods apply it to indices, shapes and tensors, and invert it.
