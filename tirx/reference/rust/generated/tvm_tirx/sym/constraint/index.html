Constraints: the facts a traversal collects about the variables in scope.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/sym/constraint.py defines. A Constraint is a premise, with a lifetime the collector defines: a BindingConstraint records the value a var is bound to, a RangeConstraint the half-open range a var lies in, and a PredicateConstraint a boolean condition its collector vouches for. A ConstraintContext holds the constraints in effect at a point of a traversal, ancestors’ first; the structural visitor and mutator of tirx::structural keep one for each value they reach.
