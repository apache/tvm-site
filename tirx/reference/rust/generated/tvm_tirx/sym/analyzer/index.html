The analyzer, its bound and modular results, and the forms of its simplifiers.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/sym/analyzer.py defines. An Analyzer keeps the facts the native analyses share. A ConstIntBound is an inclusive range [min_value, max_value], and a ModularSet is the set coeff * x + base_ for any integer x.
