tvm_tirx::ir::prim

Module expr 

Source
Expand description

Primitive operations. Each node, such as Add, LT or Select, applies one operation to PrimExpr operands, lane by lane for vectors; its new checks the operand types and derives the node’s type, without the promotion and folding of op.
