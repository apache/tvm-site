Expand description

What converts to an expression, possibly failing: anything that converts into an Expr (a node, a Var, a Rust i32, f32 and the like), and an Ex, an expression under construction, which gives the first error any of its steps raised. The constructors and functions that can fail take their expression operands as impl IntoExpr, so an Ex passes straight in and ? on their result covers it.
