Module expr

Expand description

Expressions: variables, constants, calls, tuples and tensor accesses.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/ir/expr.py defines. Expr is the base of every expression and carries its type and location. The primitive arithmetic and vector expressions are in prim. The constructors are new, or from_dtype and the like where new allocates the fields as given; they are written in Rust after the C++ constructors, with their checks, defaults and errors. The constants of the common types are IntImm::int32, IntImm::int64, IntImm::bool, FloatImm::float32 and FloatImm::float64, and an i32, i64, bool, f32 or f64 converts into an Expr as C++ converts it implicitly.
