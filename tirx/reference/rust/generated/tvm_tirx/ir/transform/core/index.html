Passes and the context they run in.

The objects python/tvm_tirx/ir/transform/core.py defines. A Pass carries its PassInfo (name and optimization level); ModulePass and Sequential are passes over a module. A PassContext holds the optimization level, the required and disabled passes, the configuration and the instruments of a run; PassContext::enter makes it current.
