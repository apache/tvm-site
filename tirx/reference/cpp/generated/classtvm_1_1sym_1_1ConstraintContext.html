inline ConstraintContext(ConstraintContext parent, int64_t parent_local_prefix)#

Construct a child capturing [0, parent_local_prefix) of its parent’s local entries. Later parent appends do not enter this child’s effective constraints.

Parameters:

  • parent – Context supplying inherited constraints.

  • parent_local_prefix – Number of parent-local entries to capture, between zero and parent->LocalSize(), inclusive.

Throws:

ffi::Error – If the prefix is outside that range.
