class ObjectVisitor : public ffi::StructuralVisitorObj#

Native visiting with exact dispatch and structural fallback.

Allocate visitors with ffi::make_object<Derived>(). Inputs are borrowed; interrupts and errors own their values. A hook controls descent into its node. One visitor must not be shared by overlapping traversals.

Subclassed by tvm::ExprVisitor

Visit a borrowed object or inline value, throwing on failure.

inline Expected<ffi::Optional<VisitInterrupt>> VisitExpected(ffi::AnyView value) noexcept#

Adapt the virtual throwing entrypoint to the structural ABI.

Note

Follows the FFI safe-call exception contract: Error retains its identity, and other standard exceptions become InternalError.

Parameters:

value – The borrowed value to visit.

Returns:

Completion, interrupt, or the caught error.
