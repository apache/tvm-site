ffi::Map<ffi::String, ffi::Array<PrimExpr>> inter#

ExecContext inter-team view at this op site.

Maps axis name (“laneid”/”warpid”/”cta_id”/”wid_in_wg”/”wgid”) to a 2-element [extent, offset] PrimExpr array. Empty map = no ExecContext tracking available (fallback for unresolved filters, pre-Phase-4 call sites, etc.); dispatchers should fall back to thread_scope.local_name in that case.
