class ConstIntBoundObj : public ffi::Object#

Inclusive constant integer bounds, representing [min_value, max_value].

The infinity sentinels represent unbounded endpoints, not finite integer values.

Public Members

int64_t min_value#

Inclusive lower endpoint, or kNegInf when unbounded.

int64_t max_value#

Inclusive upper endpoint, or kPosInf when unbounded.
