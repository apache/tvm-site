class PredicateConstraintObj : public tvm::sym::ConstraintObj#

A boolean premise. Its validity and lifetime are supplied by the collector.

Public Functions

PrimExpr condition#

The boolean predicate supplied by the collector.

bool assume_always = {false}#

Whether to assume the predicate remains true throughout the active constraint scope, including after mutations. Defaults to false. Setting true supplies the caller’s guarantee of predicate truth; it does not require referenced values to remain unchanged.
